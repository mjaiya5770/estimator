Partner with us

We partner with people and businesses whose audiences measure, build and improve things — contractors, trades, DIYers and the suppliers who serve them:

  • Tool and utility websites — if you run a free online tool our readers would find useful, and our estimators would help yours, we are interested in listing each other and cross-promoting.
  • Creators — bloggers, YouTubers and newsletter writers covering construction, remodeling and DIY: our estimators are free to link to and free to embed on your own pages.
  • Businesses — if your customers quote jobs, order materials or plan projects, we are open to integrations and joint efforts that genuinely help them.

What we offer

  • Listings — a place in our network and resource pages where there is a real fit for our users.

  • Integrations — every calculator on this site can be embedded on your website free, with no attribution required, and we are open to deeper integrations.
